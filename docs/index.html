InkTask Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026
Developer: Deraksoft
App: InkTask (com.deraksoft.inktask)

1. Summary

InkTask is a to-do app with handwriting support. Your tasks, handwriting strokes, subtasks and reminders are stored only on your device. We do not run a server, we do not create accounts, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Tasks, subtasks and handwriting strokes — saved in a local database on your device.
  • Reminders / alarms — scheduled locally on your device.
  • Subscription status — a cached copy of your plan (Free, Pro Monthly, Pro Yearly) kept locally so the app can unlock features.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it (Pro feature) and share the file yourself. Uninstalling the app removes the local data.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or usage analytics. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Google handles purchases under its own Privacy Policy. We receive only the entitlement result (whether a subscription is active) from Google Play on your device.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state — used only to communicate with Google Play Billing to verify your subscription.
  • Notifications / alarms — used only to show the task reminders you set.

6. Children's privacy

InkTask is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

7. Data security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption, backups you configure). If you enable Android's device backup, your app data may be backed up by Google according to your device settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
